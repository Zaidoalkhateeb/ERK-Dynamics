# CMS content model

All localized records should carry `language`, `translationOf`, `status`, SEO title, meta description, Open Graph media, and timestamps. All media should carry alt text, caption, rights/source, focal point, and mobile/desktop crops.

Collections: Global Settings, Pages, Products, Product Categories, Services, Solutions, Industries, Partners, Team Members, Testimonials, Case Studies, FAQs, Downloadable Resources, Navigation, Footer, SEO Metadata, Form Recipients, and Legal Pages.

Product fields: internal title, public name, slug, product number, category, manufacturer, approved relationship label, descriptions, tagline, features, benefits, applications, specifications, variants, sizes, SKU, price, currency, tax setting, inventory, delivery, warranty, certifications, accessories, hero/gallery/video/3D media, datasheet, manual, FAQs, related industries/solutions, CTA mode, publication state, and SEO fields.

Publishing rules:

- Only `published` products appear in listings, sitemap, switchers, or generated routes.
- CTA mode defaults to `quote`; ecommerce requires approved price, tax, stock, delivery, payment, return, and warranty data.
- Partnership labels and manufacturer logos remain absent until written approval exists.
- Turkish content requires editorial approval and must not publicly fall back to machine translation.
